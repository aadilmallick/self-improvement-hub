# The weekly review

If this hub has one core ritual, it is this. Everything else is an input; the
review is where input becomes decisions.

**Time:** 30 minutes. **When:** Sunday evening or Monday morning, protected.
**Where:** same place, same drink, same playlist. Boring on purpose.

## Why it works

Without a review, the week is a series of reactions. With one, you get three
things that nothing else provides:

1. **Evidence.** What actually moved, not what you remember moving.
2. **Closure.** Loose loops get closed instead of leaking attention all week.
3. **A commitment.** One priority chosen deliberately, not inherited from your inbox.

## The 30-minute agenda

### 1. Clear the decks (10 min)

- Empty the inbox to zero-ish (archive, delegate, schedule; not "read and leave").
- Process notes from the week into the [learning system](../learning/reading-and-notes).
- Look at next week's calendar and delete or move one thing you already know you will resent.

### 2. Review the scoreboard (5 min)

Three numbers, no more:

| Metric | This week | Trend |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep work sessions |  |  |
| Training sessions |  |  |
| Bedtime hit (±30 min) |  |  |

If you track ten metrics, you are logging, not reviewing.

### 3. Ask the three questions (10 min)

Write the answers. Don't think them — write them.

1. **What moved?** The thing that genuinely progressed. Be specific.
2. **What stalled, and why?** Why is usually structural: no next action, no time, no energy, no interest.
3. **What do I cut?** Somewhere in the week is a commitment that no longer earns its slot. Remove one.

### 4. Choose next week (5 min)

- **One priority:** the thing that, if it is the only thing that gets done, makes the week a success.
- **One experiment:** a single change you're testing, with a metric.
- **One protected block:** the calendar entry that defends the priority.

## The template

```markdown
## Week of ____

### Scoreboard
- Deep work: _ / _
- Training: _ / _
- Bedtime: _ / 7

### What moved

### What stalled — and why

### Cut list
- [ ]

### Next week
- Priority:
- Experiment:
- Protected block:
```

A copy-ready version lives in [Templates](../toolkit/templates#weekly-review).

## Rules of the ritual

:::note[Non-negotiable, not long]

The review can shrink to ten minutes. It cannot be skipped. A ten-minute review
keeps the loop alive; a skipped week restarts the whole thing.

:::

:::warning[Do not turn it into a planning festival]

If the review is followed by three hours of reorganizing your task manager, you
have replaced the ritual with a hobby. Thirty minutes, then close the laptop.

:::

:::tip[Keep last week's page open]

Reviewing in isolation becomes a feelings exercise. Put last week's page next to
this week's and compare commitments to reality.

:::

## Monthly and quarterly

- **Monthly (15 min):** are the three metrics trending? Is any habit ready to grow or shrink?
- **Quarterly (90 min):** see [Goals & quarters](../systems/goals-and-quarters) for the reset ritual.
